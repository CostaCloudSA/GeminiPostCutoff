# gcp-generative-media Historical & Evergreen Reference Vault

This archive vault contains foundational generative media documentation, image synthesis masterclasses, and creative toolkits for `gcp-generative-media`.

---

### 💎 Evergreen Generative Media Masterclasses
- **Foundations of Video Generation Prompting**:
  - Directing camera motion: pans, tilts, crane shots, zooms, and FPV drone trajectories.
  - Lighting and atmosphere: volumetric rays, golden hour, neon noir, and physical rendering parameters.
  - Multi-prompt storyboard decomposition vs single shot generation.

- **SynthID Digital Watermarking & Provenance**:
  - Invisible, imperceptible watermark embedding for AI-generated video and imagery.
  - Robustness against compression, cropping, and color grading.
